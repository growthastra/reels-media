Local SEO Checklist for Delhi NCR Businesses (2026 Guide)

Google Maps par top 3 mein aane ke liye step-by-step checklist — profile, website, reviews aur citations.

Delhi NCR mein har din hazaron log "near me" search karte hain. Agar aapka business Google Maps par top 3 mein nahi hai, toh customer seedha competitor ke paas jaata hai. Is guide mein hum wahi checklist share kar rahe hain jo hum apne clients ke liye use karte hain.

Local SEO koi ek-baar ka kaam nahi hai. Ye chaar cheezon par chalta hai: Google Business Profile, website ke location pages, reviews, aur directories (citations) mein same details.

Use your Google Business Profile

Sahi primary category, accurate hours, 20+ real photos aur har hafte ek post — ye chaar cheezein ranking par seedha asar daalti hain. Services aur products section bhi poora bharein.

Naam, address aur phone (NAP) har jagah bilkul same rakhein — Google, Justdial, IndiaMART, Facebook aur aapki website par.

Agar aapka clinic ya shop shift hua hai, toh purana address har jagah se hatayein. Warna Google confused hota hai aur ranking girti hai.

Location pages banayein

Har city ya area ke liye ek page — local landmarks, FAQs aur WhatsApp CTA ke saath. Isse "near me" aur "<area> + service" searches mein help milti hai.

Reviews aur replies

Har review ka reply 24 ghante mein dein. Happy customers ko WhatsApp par seedha review link bhejein.

Humare clients jinhone har hafte 3–5 naye reviews liye, unki Maps visibility 2–3 mahine mein clearly badhi.
